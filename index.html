That $8,000 Parcel Can Cost You $80,000

Cheap listings look simple. The catch is never in the headline — it's in the deed, the tax rolls, and the county office nobody calls. Here's the kind of thing I keep finding:

Catch #1

Landlocked — no legal access

The photos show a road. The deed doesn't. Without a recorded easement, you may own land you can't legally drive to.

Catch #7

The failed perc test

No perc, no septic. No septic, no house. Some counties keep failed test results on file — if you know to ask for them.

Catch #12

Back taxes ride along

In many counties, unpaid property taxes stay attached to the land — meaning the "bargain" comes with someone else's bill.

Catch #19

"Owner financing" traps

Miss one payment on some land contracts and you can lose the land and everything you've paid. It's in the fine print.

Catch #26

The flood zone surprise

That creek-side parcel may sit in a FEMA flood zone — hello insurance costs and building restrictions nobody mentioned.

Catch #33

Deed restrictions on "rural freedom"

Bought it to live free? Some rural lots carry HOA rules and deed restrictions that decide what you can build — or park.
